Canal Faceless Studio

Private command-line tool used by its own developer to upload videos and read analytics for the YouTube channels they own. It is not offered to other users.

Privacy policy

The tool runs on the developer's computer. It accesses only the developer's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, stores the OAuth token locally, and shares no data with third parties.

Terms of service

Use of this tool is restricted to its developer. No warranty is provided.
